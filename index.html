How it works

1. Text HELLO. There's no registration or app to download.

2. Add your contacts. We only collect first name and last initial.

3. Get a nudge every two weeks, then Tend checks back.

Why we built this

U.S. adults spend over 10 hours a week on social media,2 and scrolling leaves you drained. What you're missing is real, human connection with your friends and loved ones. Tend redirects that time and attention back to your relationships.
