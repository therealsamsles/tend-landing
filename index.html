Questions
How much does it cost?›

Tend is free. (Your carrier's usual message and data rates may apply.)

How often will I hear from Tend?›

About every couple of weeks for each person you add. Reply STOP anytime to pause everything.

Is it an app?›

No. Tend works entirely over text. There's an optional web dashboard at app.tendsms.com to see everyone in one place, but you never have to leave your messages.

What data do you collect?›

Just your first name, your phone number, and a first name and last initial for each person you add. Nothing else, and we never sell it. See our privacy policy.

What kind of phone do I need?›

Any phone that can send a text. No smartphone required.

How do I unsubscribe?›

Reply STOP to any message.

Who's behind Tend?›

Someone who wanted to stay in better touch with his friends and loved ones. Learn more.

Questions? Email hello@tendsms.com.
